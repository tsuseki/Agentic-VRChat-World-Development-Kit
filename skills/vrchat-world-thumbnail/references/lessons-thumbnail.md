# World-thumbnail lessons

Shot, title and retouch lessons from thumbnail review rounds. Distilled from the knowledge notes and review rounds for this topic; grep .omp/knowledge/notes/ for the evidence.

- **Put the title on one calm tone; move the camera rather than fight a dark-and-bright mix behind it** — over dark conifers against sky a navy fill vanished on the trees and a light fill's glow vanished on the sky.
- **On pale sky or haze use a dark fill with a white or ivory glow at 0.85–0.9 and no shadow** — a white title over cream grass and pale sky read weakly; dark olive with an ivory glow was accepted.
- **The name must match what the frame shows** — a name built on a plant and the sea needed that plant as the subject and the sea in view.
- **Fill the lower half with the product; tilt up 6–15° so the title sits on sky** — an empty sand foreground was rejected.
- **Pick candidates from a contact sheet of titled drafts, never from bare frames** — the title changes which frame works.
- **Preview the portal at ×0.91 horizontal squash, not "about 33 %"** — the template's drawn ellipses become circles at ×0.91; the guide's text is wrong.
- **Raise a second-language row over busy edges to `--sub-scale 0.5` with glow 0.85 / blur 0.3** — the default 0.38 was asked to be made readable twice.
- **When retouching sea streaks, take each column's sea span from its neighbours** — the streak pixels fail the sea colour test and survived on the anti-aliased horizon rows.
- **Leave the bottom band free of credit or store lines unless the owner asks** — a store-name line was removed from a whole set on request.

## lighting-baking

- [A Bakery bake that hangs forever is often the tracer blocked on an invisible error dialog](notes/bakery-hang-is-an-invisible-modal-error-box.md) — When a Bakery bake stalls with Unity idle, the ftrace tracer has usually raised a modal error box nobody can see, because Bakery launches it windowless and its fallback chain waits for an exit code that never comes.
- [A baked light placed inside a lamp mesh's bulb lights nothing](notes/lamp-light-sealed-inside-fixture-mesh.md) — Asset-pack lamps are often one solid mesh with a closed bulb, so a light placed "in the bulb" is sealed in a cavity and contributes nothing; find free volume with a ray sweep and use several lights there.
- [A build area straddling a terrain-tile boundary gets two lightmap densities](notes/terrain-tile-boundaries-split-lightmap-density.md) — On large tiled terrain where only some tiles get dedicated lightmap atlases, anything straddling a tile boundary shows a straight tonal seam and blurred shadows on the low-density side; equal texels per metre on both sides makes the seam invisible.
- [A cozy interior needs luminance contrast with the outside before it needs props](notes/interior-reads-cozy-only-if-darker-outside.md) — When the night outside measures brighter than the furniture inside, the room reads sterile; fix the contrast ordering with one uniform exposure pass before adding props.
- [A custom cutout foliage shader tagged RenderType Opaque bakes leaf cards as solid rectangles](notes/cutout-shader-needs-transparentcutout-rendertype-for-baked-shadows.md) — The Progressive lightmapper treats a shader tagged RenderType=Opaque as opaque, so every leaf card casts a rectangular baked shadow; tag the SubShader RenderType=TransparentCutout to get leaf-shaped dappled shadows.
- [A domain reload mid-bake kills Bakery's export — and Bakery can trigger it itself](notes/bakery-bake-killed-by-domain-reload.md) — Any script compile, asset refresh or native-plugin reimport during a bake kills Bakery's export coroutine, leaves stale temp objects, and can wreck lightmap bindings; know the tells and recovery steps.
- [A prop added after the last bake reads blue - check lightmapIndex before re-tinting](notes/unbaked-props-read-blue.md) — lightmapIndex -1 means no baked data, so a new prop falls back to skybox ambient and reads cold next to an identical baked one; do not re-tint the material.
- [A sealed-light audit needs to skip directional lights, inactive geometry, and lights on a face](notes/sealed-baked-light-audit-needs-three-filters.md) — A naive bounds.Contains test for lights sealed inside geometry double-counts; skip directional and inactive objects and require a margin so escaped lights on a face are not re-flagged.
- [Aim spot/wash lights with LookRotation at a target, not hand-written Euler angles](notes/aim-lights-at-a-target-not-with-euler-angles.md) — A hand-typed Euler rotation can point a light 180 degrees away from its target and nothing catches it until a full bake later.
- [Animate decor by vertex offset so it stays lightmapped](notes/animate-decor-by-vertex-offset-to-stay-lightmapped.md) — Transform/Animator motion forces a prop non-static and probe-lit, so it reads off next to baked neighbours; keep it static and move vertices in the shader instead.
- [Bakery SSS on a big fabric roof turns its underside into an area light](notes/bakery-sss-turns-large-roofs-into-emitters.md) — Per-group Bakery SSS on a large canopy can make the shaded underside brighter than sunlit ground and erase every contact shadow beneath it; scale it down with sssColor, not samples.
- [Bakery bakes the shared material emission and ignores MaterialPropertyBlocks](notes/bakery-bakes-material-emission-not-property-blocks.md) — MaterialPropertyBlock overrides never reach the material asset, so they have zero effect on a Bakery bake; baking a "fixtures off" variant means editing the material assets and restoring them afterwards.
- [Bakery ignores Unity environment lighting — add a BakerySkyLight or roofed shade bakes black](notes/bakery-needs-a-sky-light-for-ambient.md) — Unity ambientMode=Skybox is irrelevant to a Bakery bake and ambient is not added on top of lightmaps at runtime; open shade looks fine from bounce while roofed shade goes black.

## probes-reflection-and-light

- [A lamp can still read as switched on because of its albedo, not emission](notes/fixture-reads-on-because-of-its-albedo.md) — A fixture whose albedo was authored to be seen under its own bulb still reads as lit after emission is killed; judge against the key light and fix with a _Color block.
- [An object blinking between unrelated colours is outside the light-probe hull](notes/blinking-colours-probe-extrapolation-outside-hull.md) — Changing wrong colours on a moving/distant object come from light-probe extrapolation outside the LightProbeGroup volume; the Console warning names the world position.
- [Baked reflection probes capture only renderers flagged ReflectionProbeStatic](notes/reflection-probes-need-reflectionprobestatic-flag.md) — ContributeGI and ReflectionProbeStatic are independent flags; renderers without ReflectionProbeStatic are invisible to baked reflection probes, which then capture only the sky, and the tell is byte-identical cubemaps.
- [Bakery light-probe bakes can die silently and leave flat probes](notes/bakery-probe-bake-can-fail-silently.md) — A Bakery probe pass can abort without an error and leave a full-looking but flat probe set; verify probe variance after every probe bake, and close the VRChat SDK panel first.
- [Baking an alternate lighting state overwrites the shared light-probe asset](notes/alternate-lighting-state-bake-overwrites-probes.md) — A scene with day/night states has one probes asset; baking the night state leaves the day state lighting avatars with night probes — fingerprint: every probe has the same chromaticity.
- [Check receiveGI before adding lights to a black object](notes/check-receivegi-before-adding-lights.md) — A Baked light never lights a probe-lit renderer directly, so when objects read as black silhouettes despite lights aimed at them, query receiveGI first and move large ones onto the lightmap.
- [Find LightProbeGroups scene-wide and judge coverage from transformed probe positions](notes/light-probe-groups-live-outside-the-room-hierarchy.md) — Probe groups are usually parented under a lighting root, so per-room GetComponentsInChildren finds none, and a group transform-based AABB is meaningless because probe positions are local.
- [Fix a dark probe-lit prop on a lighting boundary with Renderer.probeAnchor, not extra probes](notes/probe-anchor-fixes-probe-lit-object-on-lighting-boundary.md) — A probe-lit object's look is set by the SH evaluated in the view direction; when its bounds centre lands between bright and dark probes, park an anchor where the SH matches a good sibling — no rebake needed.
- [Hit a target luminance and hue in two renders by solving a linear model](notes/two-render-solve-for-lighting-tuning.md) — When a knob enters shading linearly, measure the zero-input render and solve C = Z + m*D per channel to land a target luminance and hue on the first attempt instead of trial and error.
- [In a runtime lightmap swap, non-lightmapped props keep the old bake's light probes](notes/runtime-lightmap-swap-leaves-probe-lit-props-on-old-probes.md) — Swapping lightmapIndex changes only lightmapped renderers; probe-lit props keep the single baked probe set, and LightProbeUsage.Off binds a zero probe that renders them black.
- [Make UV2-less furniture cast baked shadows with ContributeGI + receiveGI = LightProbes](notes/uv2-less-props-can-cast-baked-shadows-with-receivegi-lightprobes.md) — Setting a UV2-less prop ContributeGI with receiveGI = LightProbes lets it cast and occlude in the lightmap while being lit by probes, with no lightmap UVs or atlas space.
- [Measure avatar lighting with a lilToon proxy sphere — light volumes only respond in play mode](notes/measure-avatar-lighting-with-a-liltoon-proxy-sphere.md) — Frame-diff a ~0.35 m lilToon sphere at each player pose; a Standard sphere has no light-volume path, and even lilToon reads no volumes in edit mode.
- [Moving a landmark does not move its reflection probe](notes/moving-an-object-leaves-its-reflection-probe-behind.md) — Reflection probes placed in the scene are not re-derived when a scripted builder moves the object they serve, and the symptom shows up elsewhere as a glossy surface reflecting raw sky.
- [Place light probes with a raycast grid; auto-placers bury probes inside geometry](notes/light-probe-placement-raycast-grid.md) — Auto-placement tools left probes inside roof shells and furniture (baked near-black), making dynamic objects go dark on furniture; a raycast-driven grid with no buried probes fixed it.
- [Probe-lit objects get all light as indirect: AO hits everything and brightness differs from lightmapped neighbours](notes/probe-lit-objects-take-all-light-as-indirect.md) — On a non-static probe-lit object, Standard's occlusion applies to its entire lighting and its albedo-to-render ratio differs from the lightmapped ground beside it — and rebaking fixes neither.
- [Relight unlit ordinary props with a synthetic SH probe, not emission](notes/unlit-props-need-a-synthetic-sh-probe-not-emission.md) — For a renderer left with no lightmap and no probe, dim emission suits only emitters; ordinary props need CustomProvided SH in a property block so they shade by normal and albedo.
- [Swapping the skybox: check what it feeds, and rotate the HDRI so its sun matches the baked light](notes/skybox-swap-and-hdri-sun-alignment.md) — A skybox swap needs no rebake only if ambient is flat and the sky light has no cubemap; align a lat-long HDRI sun to the baked directional light with a closed-form _Rotation.
- [The LightVolume authoring component keeps re-pushing its Intensity to the instance](notes/lightvolume-component-repushes-intensity.md) — VRC Light Volumes LightVolume is ExecuteAlways and re-copies its Intensity into the LightVolumeInstance on update, so setting the instance alone silently reverts; set both and treat the authoring component as source of truth.
- [Trees swapped in over baked light probes go dark on upward leaves; soften the L1 term until a re-bake](notes/swapped-trees-darken-on-stale-light-probes.md) — Probes baked with the old crowns shading them light new trees from below and darken upward leaves; until a re-bake, soften each probe's directional (L1) term so the darkest side is ≥ 30 % of the average, editing probes only through the scene's own LightingData.
- [VRC Light Volumes only reach LV-aware shaders, and dark voxels need care](notes/vrc-light-volumes-traps.md) — Light Volumes only affect LV-aware shaders (avatars, LV sample/compatible world shaders); on 3.0.1 + Bakery a scripted setup needs the hidden Bakery helpers created by hand, and a full Render bakes them.

## materials-textures-shaders

- [A .mat file stores its shader as a GUID/fileID, not a name](notes/mat-files-reference-shaders-by-guid.md) — Grepping a .mat for a shader name always finds nothing, so a diagnostic/keyed material can pass a text check and ship.
- [A lamp whose emissive faces point into an opaque dish reads switched off — swap the prop](notes/lamp-emissive-faces-hidden-reads-switched-off.md) — If a fixture's emissive texels sit on faces hidden inside a shade or dish, it looks black over a bright pool from every reachable viewpoint; check the emission map first, then swap to a prop proven lit rather than tuning.
- [A material colour multiply darkens but cannot desaturate](notes/color-tint-cannot-desaturate.md) — _Color scales R, G and B proportionally, so a grey tint only darkens; fix a garish or warm texture by swapping or desaturating the texture itself.
- [A photoscan mesh wearing another scan's texture passes every texture metric](notes/check-photoscan-mesh-texture-pairing-first.md) — A scan's albedo is only meaningful under its own mesh's UVs; a mismatched pairing reads as a corrupted smear while every texture-quality metric is normal.
- [A plant that looks dead under every light has a red-dominant albedo — replace the asset](notes/reject-red-dominant-foliage-textures.md) — Measure each foliage texture's alpha-weighted mean RGB; healthy plants are G > R, and any R > G asset reads as dried/dead at every light level.
- [A very dark _Color tint erases the albedo's own detail](notes/dark-material-tint-erases-texture-detail.md) — Pushing _Color low to get a dark tone multiplies away the texture's contrast; keep the dominant channel at or above about 0.25 and put real darkness in the albedo.
- [A view-dependent leaf edge fade must also clip the camera-depth pass, or faded cards leave white ghost slivers over shadowed ground](notes/edge-fade-must-match-camera-depth-pass.md) — In the Built-in RP the directional light's screen-space shadow collector reads the camera depth texture, which Unity draws with the ShadowCaster pass. If the leaf card's edge fade runs only in the colour pass, a faded edge-on card is gone from colour but still in depth, so the ground behind it samples the card's unshadowed depth and shows a bright leaf-shaped ghost. Apply the fade in the ShadowCaster pass only when unity_LightShadowBias is zero (camera depth); light shadow maps keep the full silhouette.
- [Audit asset-pack prop appearance by rendering, not by querying materials](notes/audit-pack-prop-appearance-by-render.md) — Pack materials are often white-tinted over a shared atlas, so colour/metallic/name sweeps find nothing; sweep a class of props by rendering it.
- [Author metals low-metallic until the bake exists, and judge them by render](notes/metallic-materials-cannot-be-judged-before-a-bake.md) — In an unbaked scene a high-metallic material has nothing to reflect and renders as one flat slab, so inspector values predict nothing about how a metal reads.
- [Authored textures import at aniso 1 / Bilinear and look smeared at grazing angles](notes/authored-textures-need-aniso-8-and-trilinear.md) — Textures written by scripts or dropped in by hand import at anisoLevel 1 and Bilinear, which smears directional grain on floors and walls and is routinely misdiagnosed as a UV problem.
- [Banding on big dark surfaces is 8-bit backbuffer quantization, fixed with a screen-space dither](notes/dark-gradient-banding-is-8bit-quantization.md) — Contour bands across a large dark gradient are usually 8-bit backbuffer quantization rather than a bake defect; no texture or rebake fixes it, a multiplicative screen-space dither does.
- [Cloned pieces sharing one mesh read as a repeating pattern; offset UVs per renderer](notes/cloned-pieces-with-identical-uvs-read-as-a-pattern.md) — N clones sharing one mesh show the same texture patch and read as wallpaper; vary _MainTex_ST offsets per renderer, and check the texture's pattern suits the surface before retiling.
- [Convert ambientCG PBR sets to Unity Standard offline, and tame _GlossMapScale](notes/ambientcg-to-standard-material-conversion.md) — Pack inverted roughness into the metallic map alpha, use the GL normal, and drop _GlossMapScale well below 1.0 or polished stone turns into a black mirror.
- [Cutout leaf cards shimmer in VR at MSAA 4x; fix with alpha to coverage plus a far-only mip bias and cutoff drop](notes/vr-leaf-shimmer-far-only-prefilter.md) — Alpha-cutout leaves ~1 px wide flicker as the head turns; MSAA alone does nothing and A2C alone ~8 %. Ramping a +0.35 mip bias and a −0.05 A2C cutoff drop in only once the leaf texture is minified past mip 1 cut flicker above a supersampled reference ~45–70 % at 6–40 m, kept crown coverage within 1 point and left near leaves untouched; measured with a sub-pixel camera-yaw metric.
- [Do not clear a MaterialPropertyBlock another system owns](notes/dont-clobber-existing-material-property-blocks.md) — Restoring with SetPropertyBlock(null) clears the block rather than restoring it; check HasPropertyBlock() in the base state before adding a renderer to a toggle list.
- [Edge-on leaf cards smear into streaks up close; fade cards at glancing angles](notes/fade-edge-on-leaf-cards-at-glancing-angles.md) — Cards seen edge-on smear into streaks within ~1 m; Crispy Foliage's 'Fade at Glancing Angles' (_EdgeFade 1) removes them with only 1–2 % pixel change at distance; 0.5 is a middle setting if edge-on cards turn into dots.
- [Fill a card crown with bigger cards and smaller-leaved sprays, never with dense clump sprays or extra cards](notes/bigger-cards-smaller-leaves-fill-crowns.md) — Dense foliage-clump sprays plus extra cards measured fuller but read as stacked textures and were rejected; drawing each spray as a bigger open branch with leaves ~1/16 of the image height and scaling cards ×1.6 kept real leaf size and gave a fuller silhouette that was approved.
- [Find which mesh is actually visible by raycast before restyling walls](notes/find-the-visible-surface-by-raycast-before-restyling.md) — Blockout wall boxes often sit behind an imported shell mesh; restyling or overlaying them changes nothing — raycast to find the visible renderer and retexture its shared material.
- [Geometry flames for a gas fire pit: small, deep orange, glow hidden between stones](notes/gas-fire-pit-emissive-flame-dimensions.md) — First-pass emissive flames come out ~3× too tall and too yellow; a gas fire pit reads right at 45–145 mm blades of deep orange with the glow card sunk out of sight.
- [Gloss painted into an albedo reads as wet plastic at any smoothness; untextured darks need lower values](notes/trim-gloss-baked-into-albedo-reads-as-plastic.md) — A trim material with varnish/piping highlights painted into its albedo looks like chrome even at low _Glossiness; swap to a matte material instead of tuning gloss.
- [Grading a generated night panorama: prompt for true night, protect lights by local contrast, elliptical stars](notes/night-sky-grading-for-generated-panoramas.md) — Orange-lit-cloud wording pulls toward dusk; protect city lights by local contrast not raw luminance; draw stars as ellipses widened by 1/cos(elevation) after upscaling.
- [Import 8K equirect HDRI skyboxes at 2048 and don't expect import settings to fix photographic softness](notes/hdri-skybox-import-resolution-and-softness.md) — An 8192×4096 HDRI needs a 2048 cubemap face to carry its detail (1024 looks blurry through big windows); softness baked into the photo needs real geometry for a crisp skyline.
- [Interior mapping fakes rooms behind windows with a shader, no geometry](notes/interior-mapping-fake-rooms-behind-windows.md) — For lit windows on distant or unreachable buildings, use an interior-mapping shader (per-pixel ray vs. regularly spaced wall/floor/ceiling planes) instead of modelling rooms; it gives perspective-correct depth for one flat quad.
- [Metallic materials render black in sealed dark rooms but are the only thing that reads against a bright probe](notes/metallic-depends-on-what-the-reflection-probe-sees.md) — Standard metals have no diffuse term, so whether high metallic rescues or kills a dark prop depends entirely on what the reflection probe captures.
- [Non-power-of-two textures silently lose aspect ratio or compression — import with npotScale = ToLarger](notes/npot-textures-lose-aspect-and-compression.md) — npotScale=ToNearest rounds each axis independently (stored aspect changes), and NPOT + mipmaps blocks DXT (RGB24 at ~9x VRAM); any POT rescale compresses, and the quad — sized from the source file's aspect, never from Texture.width/height — stretches it back.
- [On Mochie Glass, any non-zero _Roughness blurs the whole world behind the pane](notes/mochie-glass-roughness-enables-screen-blur.md) — Mochie/Glass gates a blurred grabpass on _Roughness > 0 and also uses it to pick a blurrier reflection mip, showing up as 'distant terrain is blurry' and 'glass has no reflections'.
- [On Standard shaders a bound map silently overrides the scalar you edit](notes/standard-shader-maps-override-scalars.md) — Smoothness, metallic and emission edits on Standard materials silently no-op when a gloss map, albedo-alpha smoothness, stale keyword or emission mask is in play; inspect maps and keywords, and verify on a render.
- [One-atlas tree layout: four 3:4 portrait spray cells plus a bark strip that repeats in V](notes/one-atlas-tree-layout-portrait-cells-and-bark-strip.md) — A square 2048² atlas with four 3:4 portrait spray cells and a vertical bark strip (wrap U Clamp / V Repeat) lets any shader tile bark with plain UVs in one material; cards take the cell aspect so sprays aren't squeezed; a square bark tile with cut UVs cost +47–92 % tris or 4× blurrier bark.
- [Recolour one material class in a shared atlas with an HSV saturation mask](notes/recolour-shared-atlas-by-saturation-mask.md) — To retint wood in a furniture atlas without changing its fabric, mask by saturation and remap the masked mean to the target albedo's mean; a _Color tint multiplies everything.
- [Scaled primitives sharing a material get different texture periods — fix with MaterialPropertyBlock](notes/scaled-primitive-texture-period-mpb.md) — Unit cubes scaled to different lengths with one material show a different world texture period each; set _MainTex_ST per renderer from world size via MaterialPropertyBlock.
- [SetColor converts gamma to linear: brightness fits must follow the sRGB curve](notes/setcolor-gamma-conversion-breaks-linear-fits.md) — In a Linear project SetColor applies the exact sRGB-to-linear curve, so rendered luminance scales as ~k^2.2 above the 0.04045 knee and linearly below it; pick the right regime and raise search ceilings since albedo can exceed 1.
- [SetPropertyBlock replaces the whole block and covers every submesh](notes/setpropertyblock-replaces-not-merges.md) — A MaterialPropertyBlock replaces the renderer block rather than merging, so a renderer in two passes keeps only the last, and one per-renderer block hits every submesh, so solving against sharedMaterial moves the wrong element.
- [Surface-shader alphatest pragma can make cutout foliage blend with the framebuffer](notes/alphatest-pragma-blends-cutout-against-background.md) — A `alphatest:_Cutoff` surface-shader pragma left cutout leaves blending with whatever was behind them, so leaves in front of a bright HDR sky burned to white; use an explicit clip() instead.
- [Use radial fog applied from your own shader uniforms — view-space fog makes edge "walls" and mirrors drop fog](notes/fog-must-be-radial-and-shader-driven-for-mirrors.md) — Built-in view-space fog thins toward screen edges so radial boundaries show as a peripheral wall, and VRChat mirrors render without Unity fog state; apply ExpSq fog unconditionally with radial distance from shader uniforms.
- [Use world-projected UVs for uniform tiling and fit one texture repeat to the wall height](notes/world-projected-uvs-and-fitting-repeats-to-wall-height.md) — Generate floors/walls as single meshes with UVs = world position / repeat size so density is uniform; for panelled wall textures set scale.y = 1/height and offset so one repeat spans the wall exactly.
- [When repurposing a pack material, clear every map, not just _MainTex](notes/repurposing-pack-materials-clear-every-map.md) — On atlas materials the roughness and normal maps carry the design itself, so replacing only the albedo leaves the old pattern visible, and a bound _SpecGlossMap makes the smoothness slider dead.
- [With _EMISSION disabled, an _EmissionColor MaterialPropertyBlock silently does nothing](notes/emission-keyword-off-ignores-property-block.md) — The forward pass branches on the _EMISSION keyword, so a property-block emission colour on a material with the keyword off applies, reads back correctly, and never renders.
- [World-space TextMesh on the default font material draws through all geometry](notes/textmesh-default-font-material-draws-through-walls.md) — The built-in text shader uses ZTest Always, so TextMesh labels show through walls and floors; use a copy with ZTest LEqual.

## geometry-meshes-uvs

- [A per-prop triangle floor must clamp every LOD, and check which LOD is on screen](notes/lod-tri-floor-must-clamp-every-lod.md) — LOD ratios multiply straight through a floor applied only to LOD0, and LOD0 on small props stops rendering a few metres out — so the broken LOD is what every screenshot shows.
- [A prop that renders as a blank slab is facing away — measure the submesh normal](notes/featureless-prop-check-facing-before-material.md) — When a prop renders featureless but materials, textures and bounds all check out, measure the content submesh's mean local normal; it is probably presenting its back.
- [A prop with a wide flat foot cannot be tilted as a whole object](notes/tilt-base-plate-props-by-hinging-the-mesh.md) — Rotating a prop that has a wide base plate always drives a plate corner underground; hinge the mesh above a joint instead.

- [Area-weighted lightmap unwraps starve tree bark because alpha-cut leaf cards count as full quads](notes/tree-lightmap-uv-transparency-trap.md) — An automatic lightmap unwrap sizes charts by 3D surface area and ignores transparency, so thousands of mostly see-through leaf quads take the lightmap and thin bark limbs get about one texel each; ship a hand-built lightmap UV in the FBX that gives bark its own budget.
- [Bad textures" on a hand-built mesh is usually material tiling anisotropy; fix it in UV1](notes/fix-texture-stretch-via-effective-repeats-per-metre.md) — Measure effective repeats per metre per axis (mesh UV per metre x material tiling); fix anisotropy with a per-axis world box projection in UV1, leaving shared materials and UV2 untouched.
- [Check wall-panel UV handedness against siblings and a text panel](notes/wall-panel-uv-handedness-sibling-check.md) — Deciding a panel's outward face by raycast fails silently on collider-less walls, mirroring some panels; assert sibling panels on the same wall share handedness and validate with a panel showing text.
- [EditorUtility.CopySerialized into an existing Mesh asset keeps rendering the old geometry](notes/copyserialized-into-existing-mesh-renders-stale.md) — Rewriting a mesh asset in place with CopySerialized updates its CPU data but the renderer keeps drawing the old mesh; write a new asset instead.
- [Fix texture stretch with a UV pass, never by rebuilding geometry](notes/fix-texture-stretch-with-uvs-not-geometry.md) — Stretched textures from non-unit localScale are a UV defect; a "rebuild at world scale" sweep filtered by a property also replaced spheres, cylinders and rotated parts with axis-aligned boxes.
- [Hand-rolled mesh builders can wind triangles against their normals — make them self-correcting](notes/scripted-mesh-builder-winding-check.md) — A scripted box/quad helper can emit every triangle inside-out while normals look correct; test winding against a primitive Cube control and make the builder flip inverted pairs itself — the test misreports imported FBX meshes.
- [Hard flat bands on a lightmapped surface can be duplicate triangles, not normals](notes/hard-bands-on-baked-surface-duplicate-triangles.md) — Coincident duplicate triangles force one UV2 chart per triangle and z-fight with different baked values, painting hard bands that look like missing smooth shading.
- [Leaf-card cull rarely passes on dense volume crowns; budget an unculled card count](notes/card-cull-rarely-passes-on-dense-volume-crowns.md) — Removing even 5 % of cards from a dense spindle/ellipsoid crown moved the silhouette 2–22 cm against a 0.3 cm allowance, so plan the unculled card count inside the budget; branch-structured crowns still pass at 10–20 %.
- [Make water sheets wider than the basin and bury their edges](notes/bury-water-sheet-edges-under-terrain.md) — Spiky or straight water edges are the water mesh's own polygon boundary showing; extend the sheet up the banks so the terrain contour forms the shoreline, and verify by minimum edge burial.

- [Pieces cut from a photoscan are hollow shells that fool bounding-box tests](notes/photoscan-pieces-are-hollow-shells.md) — Fragments cut from a photoscan surface are open shells with no thickness, and a curled shell has a chunky bounding box, so gate them on volume divided by surface area, not bbox aspect.

- [Check the live scene before acting on each review finding — some may already be fixed](notes/verify-review-findings-against-live-scene.md) — A reviewer's screenshots can predate fixes already applied; dump the named objects' current state before editing, and record already-closed findings with the proving measurement.

- [Get every generated or placed asset independently reviewed before calling it done](notes/get-every-generated-asset-independently-reviewed.md) — Scripted placement passes "succeed" while producing obvious visual defects, so every asset or placement must be screenshot-checked and then rated by an independent reviewer before it is reported finished.

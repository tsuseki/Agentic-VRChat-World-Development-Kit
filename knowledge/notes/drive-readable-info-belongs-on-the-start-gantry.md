---
title: Put drive-readable info on the start gantry, not a trackside board
topic: placement-layout-design
tags: [signage, racetrack, readability, gantry, vehicles]
summary: A long trackside board cannot face oncoming drivers without overhanging the road; the start/finish gantry is the one surface dead-centre in the forward view.
---
**Symptom:** asked to rotate a 15 m trackside timing board so drivers can read it, every rotation either overhangs the asphalt or stays ~50° off-axis.

**Cause:** to face a driver the board must be near-perpendicular to the track, which swings its length across the road. A search over 105–145° rotations and the whole shoulder found nothing better than 55° off-axis.

**Fix / rule:**
- Hang the readout under the start/finish gantry sign band: drivers look straight at it; it reads cleanly from 30–60 m.
- If the free vertical band is small (< 2 m), go **wide and single-row** (e.g. 20 m × 1.9 m strip, ~1.05 m digits) rather than two stacked rows of small text.
- Keep parked props clear of gantry leg towers or they get cut in half from the driving line.

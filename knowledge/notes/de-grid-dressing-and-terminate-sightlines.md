title: De-grid furniture clusters and end entrance sightlines on a focal piece
topic: placement-layout-design
tags: [dressing, furniture, composition, focal point, jitter, lounge]
summary: Scripted dressing lands on a perfect grid; rotate clusters a few degrees, jitter small items, and put a focal object where the entrance sightline ends.
---
**How:**
- Rotate furniture clusters ±6–12° around their centres and jitter bottles/plants/candles so the room doesn't read as a grid.
- Put a focal piece (centre table under a chandelier, art, a window) where the entrance sightline ends. No mirrors as focal pieces: VRChat has personal mirrors.
- Hang chandeliers so stems reach the ceiling; check clones keep their light components.
- Cover floor seams at doorways with a thin threshold strip (3 cm, collider off).
- Check parent/child active consistency: an inactive deck under active railings/furniture exposed a hole through to backfaces from one doorway.
- An empty centre can be deliberate social space — say so in the reviewer brief.

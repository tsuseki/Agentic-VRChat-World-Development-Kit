title: Daylit outdoor areas need no artificial lights — tell the reviewer up front
topic: lighting-baking

summary: "Enough light sources, each with a fixture mesh" is an interior criterion; in a sunlit exterior, judge only the baked daylight and filter out reviewer requests for lanterns.
---
**Rule:**
- Interior criteria such as "enough light sources" and "every light needs a visible fixture (chandelier, lantern, sconce)" apply to interiors only.
- In a sunlit exterior the baked directional light already lights everything; extra point lights are wasted bake cost and visual noise. Judge illumination purely on the baked daylight result (no black shade, not overexposed). If shade bakes black, fix the sun's indirect contribution (see [sun-bake-to-indirect-off-makes-outdoor-shade-black](sun-bake-to-indirect-off-makes-outdoor-shade-black.md)), not by adding lights.
- When briefing a scene reviewer on an outdoor area, state that it is daylit and that light-count recommendations are out of scope.

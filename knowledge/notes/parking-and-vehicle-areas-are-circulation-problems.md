---
title: A parking lot is a circulation problem, not a packing problem
topic: placement-layout-design
tags: [parking, vehicles, layout, aisle, bays]
summary: Every vehicle stall must reach the exit without crossing another stall; plan aisles and a turnaround first, then bays.
---
**Symptom:** a dense bay layout where back-row cars must drive through other parked cars, with no drivable entrance or exit.

**Fix / rule (measured layout that worked):**
- Two rows of nose-in stalls sharing one **~9.25 m aisle**; bay pitch **5.2 m** so the widest vehicle (a tank) fits.
- Leave a large open apron (46 m in a 104 × 52 m pad) as drive-in and turnaround.
- Keep the row extendable in one direction for future vehicles.
- Furniture under a post-grid canopy: place tables at **post-grid midpoints** so nothing clips a post.
- For forgiving vehicle events with very different vehicles, keep the road **uniformly wide** (35 m was used), with no narrow sections.
